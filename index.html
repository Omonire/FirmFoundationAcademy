Our Programmes

Academic Excellence.

A robust curriculum from Nursery through Junior Secondary, tailored for every stage of your child's development.

Nursery Section

Play-based learning, phonics, creative arts, and motor skills development for ages 2–6. Preschool, KG1, KG2, KG3.

Primary Section

Blended Nigerian & British curriculum for ages 6–12. Core subjects, ICT, and holistic development. Primary 1–6.

Junior Secondary

Rigorous academic preparation for ages 12–15. Subject specialists, critical thinking, and exam excellence. JSS1–JSS3.
