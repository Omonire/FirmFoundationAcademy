Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the academic session begin?

Our academic session typically begins in September. However, we accept applications and enrolments throughout the year subject to availability of spaces.

Is there an entrance exam for all classes?

Yes, all new students take a brief age-appropriate assessment. For nursery and early primary, this is a simple observational activity. For primary 4 and above, it involves English and Mathematics.

Do you offer mid-term or mid-year enrolment?

Yes, we accept mid-term enrolments subject to available spaces. Contact us to check availability for the specific class you are interested in.

What is your school hours schedule?

School hours run from 8:00 AM to 2:30 PM Monday to Thursday and 8:00 AM to 1;00 PM on Friday. After-school lessons is available on request — contact us for details.

How do I get the school fees breakdown?

Please contact us directly via phone, WhatsApp, or by visiting the school. Our admissions team will provide the full fee structure for your child's specific class.
